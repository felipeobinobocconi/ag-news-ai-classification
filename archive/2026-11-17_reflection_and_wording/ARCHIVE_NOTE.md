## Why these versions were superseded

1. **Reflection on the three agent mistakes.** The live report now explains each
   mistake in terms of what happened, the likely mechanism (labelled as
   interpretation), detection and its limits, the correction, and a practice for
   next time.
2. **Descriptive category interpretations.** The live report now reports the
   within-pool split (Business 13/52, Sci/Tech 0/22), the World rate (22/73) and
   the human, AI and dataset category distributions, with explicit limits on
   what these descriptions do not show.
3. **Analysis-model identification.** The live report records the OpenCode
   session model identifiers for the workflow and autonomous runs.
4. **Fresh-session clarification.** The live report states that the autonomous
   analysis used a fresh session and the same frozen classifications, without
   repeating the Gemini classification.
5. **Assistance wording and authorship.** The product name was replaced with
   "an auxiliary AI assistant" in current reader-facing documents, and the
   student's authorship of the 200 labels and 38 review judgments is stated
   explicitly. The brief's dataset-access disclosure was added.
6. **Housekeeping.** The empty row in `mistakes.md` was removed, and two
   prospective `AGENTS.md` rules were added.
7. **Repository state.** The live documents now record that a local Git
   repository pre-dates this revision and that its existing commit tracks the
   earlier archive copies. No commit, push or history rewrite was made in this
   revision.

No classification, codebook rule, frozen input or numerical result changed.
The historical files that contain the auxiliary assistant's product name are
retained here and in `archive/2026-11-14_documentation_corrections/`; they are
excluded from the reader-facing delivery set. The pre-existing Git commit also

- **An auxiliary AI assistant helped review the preparation annotations** (the 20
  texts in the preparation exercise) and refine the classification codebook; the
  clarifications recorded in codebook version 0.3 and the frozen version 1.0
  grew out of that review.
- The student completed the **initial preparation labels before receiving

- No model versions, settings or verbatim prompt/output logs of that
  assistance are available, so none are claimed here; this note records only
  the fact, timing and scope of the assistance.
- **Scope:** the assistance concerned preparation texts only. No evaluation
  text was discussed with, shown to, or classified by any AI before the manual
  evaluation labels are complete and frozen (plan.md steps 3–5).
- Consequence for the analysis: the preparation cases are **not independent
  evaluation observations**; they informed the codebook and are excluded from
  the evaluation sample by design.
